# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of positive behavior include empathy, respectful feedback, accepting responsibility, and
focusing on what is best for the community. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, trolling,
insults, sexualized attention, publishing private information, or other conduct inappropriate in a
professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and contributions
that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct. Report conduct concerns privately through GitHub's
available maintainer contact mechanisms. Maintainers will respect the reporter's privacy and apply
responses proportionate to the impact and recurrence of the behavior.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1:
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html
